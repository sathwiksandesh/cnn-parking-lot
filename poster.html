📋 Description
CNN Parking Lot is a vision-based occupancy detection system that processes overhead surveillance images and video feeds to classify every parking bay as Free or Occupied in real-time. It uses an ultra-lightweight custom CNN (only 63,137 parameters) trained on the CNR-EXT dataset (144,958+ crops across 9 camera views, sunny to rainy conditions). YOLOv8-nano localises vehicle candidates and the CNN verifies each slot with a calibrated 0.30 probability threshold. A 3-frame temporal rolling filter eliminates flicker, and all results are served through an asynchronous FastAPI microservice and an interactive Streamlit dashboard.
🌐 Real-World Use
✔ Real-time free/occupied parking bay detection from CCTV
✔ Driver guidance to nearest available parking slot
✔ Reduce urban traffic congestion (up to 30% from cruising)
✔ No road-drilling — zero in-ground physical sensors needed
✔ GDPR-compliant: no license plates or faces captured
✔ Works across all weather: sun, rain, shadow, overcast
